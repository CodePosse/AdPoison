Step 03 · Reset

Kill your ad IDs

Your phone and computer hand apps a unique advertising ID: a name tag that follows you between apps and data brokers. Delete it, then switch off personalization at the big platforms. Tick each box as you go; progress is saved in this browser only.

Meta builds most of your ad profile from activity outside its apps: the Meta Pixel on shopping sites, app SDKs, and customer lists that advertisers upload. Why delete instead of reset? A reset gives you a fresh ID, and new tracking starts immediately. Deleting (Android 12+) or blocking tracking requests (iOS) hands apps a meaningless all-zero ID, so there's nothing new to profile. Further reading: Pixel Defence's guide and NordVPN's walkthrough.
