We collect nothing

A privacy tool that tracked you would be a sick joke. So this site doesn't.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics, pixels, heatmaps or fingerprinting.
  • No cookies.
  • No accounts, forms or server-side code. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • No third-party requests: fonts and images are served from this site.

What stays in your browser

To remember your choices, the site uses your browser's localStorage, prefixed adpoison:. It never leaves your device:

  • Chaff generator settings and a lifetime hop counter.
  • Hosts builder selections and custom domains.
  • Checklist ticks on the reset and hygiene pages.
  • The fingerprint check's previous scan: one short hash per signal and the time of the scan, never the raw values. The check itself runs only in your tab and sends nothing.

Clear it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or press the button below.

The chaff window

When you run the generator, the extra window visits the destinations you choose (for example Google or Amazon). Those sites see the visit like any other, under their own privacy policies. That's the point: they receive decoy activity. Ad Poison itself never sees what happens in that window.

Your host

Whoever hosts this copy of Ad Poison may keep standard server access logs (IP address, time, page requested). Check their policy, or run your own copy: the source is open.
